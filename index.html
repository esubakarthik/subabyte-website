Play with Krypto

An interactive cryptography learning lab for iOS. Explore ciphers, hashing, digital signatures, authentication, blockchain demos, and build-your-own encryption pipelines — with calculated values at every step so you see why verification succeeds or fails.

  • Education
  • iOS
  • On-device
  • No account required

Inside the app

Classic ciphers (Caesar, Vigenère, Playfair) through modern AES and RSA encryption labs.

Digital signatures and authentication: RSA challenge–response login, TOTP authenticator codes, and HMAC challenge–response.

Blockchain and cryptocurrency sandboxes — including optional nearby peer sessions on your local network.

Not a wallet. Demo keys and currencies exist only for learning.

NightOwl Responders

Privacy-first peer-to-peer chat for people on call. Private invite-only groups, short-lived topics, and encrypted phone-to-phone messages — with no NightOwl server holding your history.

  • P2P
  • On-call
  • No chat backend
  • iOS

Designed for cover shifts

Ask colleagues for help from a personal phone — topics expire after 24, 48, or 72 hours.

iCloud is only the doorbell so peers can find each other; chat never sits in our cloud.

Optional on-device leak alerts, notices without replies, and on-call POC alerts that never include message content.

Unofficial. Not an NHS, hospital, or employer system — keep mandated work on approved channels.
