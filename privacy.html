Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 July 2026
App: Play with Krypto (iOS)
Data controller: SUBABYTE LTD
Registered address: Registered in England and Wales (registered office available on Companies House)

This Privacy Policy explains how SUBABYTE LTD (“we”, “us”) handles information in connection with the Play with Krypto mobile application and this website. Contact: contact@subabyte.co.uk.

Play with Krypto is an educational cryptography app. It is not a cryptocurrency wallet, exchange, bank, or payment service. Demo keys and demo “currencies” are for learning only.

1. Summary

  • We do not require you to create an account to use the app.
  • Learning labs run on your device. We do not operate a backend that receives your lab text, keys, or codes.
  • Some preferences and saved recipes are stored on your device only.
  • An optional “nearby friends” feature uses your local network to talk to nearby devices you choose to join — not our servers.
  • We do not sell personal data.

2. Information we process

Category Examples Where it stays
App preferences Whether you have completed the intro On your device
Saved learning content Custom encodings; saved encryption recipes On your device
Demo crypto material Keys, secrets, challenges, OTP codes you generate in labs On your device (not uploaded by us)
Nearby session data (optional) Display name, public keys, demo ledger messages with peers Local network / peer devices only
Support email Whatever you write when you email us Our email inbox, to respond to you

The current shipping app does not use advertising identifiers, analytics SDKs, crash reporters that phone home, accounts, or cloud sync operated by us.

3. Local Network / nearby friends

If you use the cryptocurrency lab’s nearby-friends mode, iOS may ask for Local Network permission. The app uses Bonjour / Multipeer Connectivity so nearby devices can join a temporary educational session. If you never use that feature, the rest of the app does not need it.

4. Sharing you initiate

If you share a custom encryption recipe via the system Share Sheet, content leaves your device only because you chose a destination (Messages, Mail, Files, etc.). Shared recipes are designed to include prompts for secrets, not the secret values themselves.

5. Legal bases (UK GDPR)

Where UK GDPR applies, we rely on:

  • Legitimate interests — operating and supporting an educational app and website.
  • Contract / steps at your request — responding to support emails you send us.
  • Consent — where required for optional device permissions (e.g. Local Network).

6. Retention

  • On-device data remains until you delete it in-app (where available) or delete the app.
  • Support emails are kept only as long as needed to handle your request and meet basic record-keeping needs.

7. Your rights (UK)

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to certain processing, and to complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO). To exercise rights related to information you sent us by email, contact contact@subabyte.co.uk.

8. Children

The app is a general educational tool. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children via the app. If you believe a child has sent us personal information by email, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Third parties

Apple may process data related to App Store distribution, device services, and (if you purchase) payments under Apple’s own policies. We do not control Apple’s processing.

10. International transfers

On-device app data stays on your device. If you email us, your message is processed by our email provider. We will take reasonable steps appropriate to the tools we use.

11. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change. Continued use of the app after changes means you should review the updated policy.
