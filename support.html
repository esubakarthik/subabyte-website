Quick FAQ

Is this a real crypto wallet?

No. Play with Krypto is an educational sandbox. Demo currencies, keys, and nearby ledgers are for learning how cryptography and consensus ideas work — not real money.

Why does the app ask for Local Network?

Only for the optional “nearby friends” cryptocurrency experiment, so devices on the same local network can join a temporary learning session. You can ignore that feature if you prefer.

Where is my data stored?

Lab work stays on your device. See our Privacy Policy for details.
