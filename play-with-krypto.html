Project · iOS · Education

Play with Krypto

Learn how cryptography works by experimenting with it. An interactive lab for students, developers, and curious minds who want the ideas to feel concrete — not abstract.

Educational software only. Play with Krypto is not a cryptocurrency wallet, exchange, bank, or payment service. Demo keys and demo currencies exist solely for learning.

What you will explore

Classic & modern ciphers

Caesar, Vigenère, Playfair, Rail Fence, XOR, AES-256, and RSA encryption labs with step-by-step explanations.

Hashing & signatures

SHA-256 fingerprints and P-256 digital signatures with calculated verification values you can inspect.

Authentication labs

RSA challenge–response login, TOTP authenticator codes (RFC 6238), and HMAC challenge–response.

Blockchain & cryptocurrency demos

Build chains, try tamper attacks, simulate ledgers, and optionally connect with nearby friends on your local network.

Build your own encryption

Combine reversible steps into custom recipes, test every stage, save, and share the recipe (secrets stay as prompts only).

Calculated values everywhere

See hashes, fingerprints, signatures, and pass/fail reasons — so you understand why verification works or breaks.

Privacy & data

  • No account required to use the app.
  • Lab content, keys, and saved recipes stay on your device.
  • We do not operate a backend that receives your lab data.
  • Optional nearby mode uses your local network only (Multipeer Connectivity) — never our servers.

Who it is for

Anyone learning computer science, security fundamentals, or cryptography for the first time — and anyone who wants a safe sandbox to break things on purpose and see what happens.
