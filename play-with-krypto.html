Play with Krypto

Learn how cryptography works by experimenting with it — an interactive lab for students, curious builders, and anyone who wants the ideas to feel concrete.

Educational software only. Play with Krypto is not a wallet, exchange, bank, or payment service.

What it covers

  • Classic and modern ciphers
  • Hashing and digital signatures
  • Authentication labs (RSA login challenges, TOTP, HMAC)
  • Blockchain and cryptocurrency learning demos
  • Build-your-own reversible encryption recipes
