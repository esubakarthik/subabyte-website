Topic Chat

An unofficial, topic-based peer-to-peer chat app. Organise conversations into focused topics inside each room — and let them disappear after ten days, with no company database holding your messages.

Coming soon. Topic Chat is in active development. Features and timing may change before release. This page describes our current design intent.

The idea

Most chat apps centralise your conversations on a server. Topic Chat takes a different approach: rooms connect devices directly, conversations are organised by topic within each room, and message history lives on participants’ devices — not in a SubaByte database.

Planned features

Topic-based rooms

Create chat rooms and add topics inside them — so discussions stay focused instead of becoming one endless thread.

10-day topic expiry

Topics are designed to fade: content within a topic is automatically removed after ten days, encouraging lighter, more ephemeral conversations.

No backend database

We do not plan to operate a central server that stores your chat history. Room data is intended to remain on user devices.

Peer-to-peer connectivity

Devices connect directly where possible, reducing reliance on a permanent cloud archive of your messages.

Local privacy assistant

A planned on-device agent to help flag potentially confidential information — such as passwords or account numbers — before you post.

Unofficial & independent

Topic Chat is an independent SubaByte product — not affiliated with any existing messaging platform or brand.

Privacy-first by architecture

  • No SubaByte-operated database for chat content.
  • Messages and room data stored on your device, under your control.
  • Ephemeral topics — content designed to expire, not accumulate forever.
  • Local-only checks before sending — confidential-data warnings processed on device.

What we are still deciding

Exact peer discovery, encryption details, and group-size limits are being refined during development. A full privacy policy for Topic Chat will be published before App Store release.
